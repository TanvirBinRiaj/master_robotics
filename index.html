How to read this book

You do not need a university degree, a big budget, or perfect English. You need three simple habits:

  1. Read one chapter at a time. Each chapter is short and stands on its own.
  2. Pause at every example. Say the idea out loud in your own words. If you can explain it, you understand it.
  3. Build something early. The book ends with a full robot project. The sooner your hands are busy, the faster your brain learns.
Plain-English promise

Every technical word is explained the first time it appears, and again in the glossary. Short sentences. Everyday examples. No jargon without a translation.
